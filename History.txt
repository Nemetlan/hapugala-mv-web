# History of Ga/Hapugala Maha Vidyalaya
### A Complete Historical Account

---

## Origins and Foundation (1902)

The Ga/Hapugala Maha Vidyalaya stands as an institution of 117 years of proud history, situated in proximity to the Gin Ganga river, near Komaliya, in the Galu Pura (Galle) district of Sri Lanka. Its founding is inseparably tied to the philanthropic vision of a wealthy landowner of Galle, who, wishing to bestow an enduring legacy upon the rural children of Hapugala, approached and challenged a man of means and civic spirit — **Andyess George Jayasundara Mudalidu** of Hapugala — to establish a school for the village children.

Moved by that noble inspiration, on **2nd January 1902**, Andyess George Jayasundara Mudalidu officially took steps to provide education to the children of the village by founding a humble ground-level school. The land upon which the institution was established, measuring **2 roods and 1 perch**, had previously been used for the cultivation of coconut and areca nut trees. Before this establishment, no formal educational institution existed for the village children, and the land served purely agricultural purposes.

The school commenced its operations in a **newly constructed coconut-thatched shed**, modest in appearance but monumental in purpose. It opened its doors with approximately **two dozen students**, drawing the young children of Hapugala who would otherwise have had no access to formal education.

The school was inaugurated on an auspicious day — the birth anniversary of its founder George — and was formally named the **"Rajagisheka Baudha Miga Padyashalawa"** (Rajagisheka Buddhist Mixed School), reflecting both its religious character and its founding spirit.

---

## The First Principal and Early Staff

The school's **first principal** was **Mr. Wataraka Padinivi Palgasinha**, a man of distinguished service. The first teacher on record was **Mr. Gin Padinivi Mendis**. At its inception, the institution had only **4 teachers** and **28 students** — a small but determined community united by the pursuit of knowledge.

## The Era of Mr. Palgasinha and the Early Decades

Under the founding principal **Mr. Palgasinha**, the school established its academic roots. Several subsequent principals — including **Mr. F.D.Y. da Silva**, **Mr. K.L.P. Dyas**, **Mr. F. Vikumasinha**, **Mr. Sunil Mendis**, and others — built upon this foundation, each contributing to the gradual development of the institution through the mid-twentieth century.

---

## The Transformative Era of Mr. S. Pethangoda (1978–1992)

The year **1978** marked a turning point in the school's history. On **28th March 1978**, **Mr. S. Pethangoda** assumed duties as the new principal and ushered in an era of remarkable progress.

Under his stewardship:
- The teaching staff expanded to **45 members**.
- Student enrollment surged to **over 800**, organized into **5 parallel streams**.
- The school was expanded to accommodate classes from **Grade 1 through Grade 13**, with **25 classes** in total.
- The school was elevated up to **Grade 10** initially, and subsequently **Grade 13**.
- The **Arts and Commerce streams** at the **Advanced Level (A/L)** were introduced, marking the school's evolution into a fully-fledged secondary and collegiate institution.

Mr. Pethangoda also introduced mandatory examination participation for teachers, while simultaneously ensuring students were prepared for public examinations. He initiated the **A/O (Ordinary Level) Commerce stream** alongside the existing General Level, broadening academic choices. Student welfare and welfare systems were also formalized during this era, and a framework of school discipline was firmly established.

An important cultural addition during this period was the establishment of a **Music Room**, created through the contributions of musician **Mr. B.P. Seneratna** and the guidance of **Suva Mahathiya**, which served to nurture the aesthetic sensibilities of the students.

During a transitional period, **Mr. K.R. Rathnawira** served as Acting Principal, while the Provincial Education Director administered oversight through the Bopage Poddala Kotwasa office.

---

## The Acting Tenure of Mr. Piyasena Game's (1992 onwards)

In **1992**, **Mr. Piyasena Game's** assumed the role of Acting Principal and subsequently served in a substantive capacity. His tenure saw the school achieve **new academic milestones**, including the construction of a **new building with four halls**, significantly expanding the school's physical infrastructure. His administration placed particular emphasis on university access, enabling more students to pursue higher education than ever before.

---

## The Era of Mr. S. Kulasinha (2000–2003)

On **20th June 2000**, after a period of administrative transition involving teacher representation and signature campaigns, **Mr. Piyasena Game's** handed over administration to **Mr. S. Kulasinha**, who formally assumed duties as principal.

Mr. Kulasinha undertook an ambitious programme of institutional development:
- **Arts and Commerce** dual streams at the **Advanced Level** were formally consolidated and expanded.
- Remote communication infrastructure was obtained.
- A **water supply system** was established.
- A **Primary Section Unit** was formally structured.
- A **Young Children's Unit** (Early Childhood section) was introduced.
- The **modern library building** was reinforced and expanded.
- A **Computer Unit** was established, bringing the school into the digital age.
- The principal's office was established in its current location.
- Grade 7 classrooms were commenced.
- The school's **foundation stone for a library** was laid.
- Childcare support services were initiated.

On **22nd September 2003**, Mr. Kulasinha departed, and **Mr. T.P. Dharmadasa** was appointed as an Acting Principal on a temporary government assignment basis. This transitional arrangement lasted until **13th February 2004**, when a new permanent appointment was made.

---

## The Era of Mr. Siril Kalansuriya (2004 onwards)

**Mr. Siril Kalansuriya** assumed the principalship of the school on **13th February 2004**, bringing with him a vision of modernization and infrastructure excellence.

Key achievements under his leadership:
- **Six Grade 6 classrooms** were successfully established, doubling the school's Grade 6 accommodation.
- A **Computer Laboratory** and a **new Library building** valued at **7 lakhs (Rs. 700,000)** were constructed and equipped, greatly enriching student resources.
- In **2007**, a **Buddha statue** was erected on the school premises, commissioned as a symbol of the students' devotion and the school's spiritual heritage.

During this era, the school's students began distinguishing themselves at **national and international competitions**, bringing recognition and prestige to the institution.

---

## The Transformative Leadership of Mr. K.P.K. Wipul Milinda Padikkorale (2010–2017)

On **3rd September 2010**, **Mr. K.P.K. Wipul Milinda Padikkorale** assumed duties as principal, described in institutional records as having given the school "a new life" and inaugurating "a new era."

Under his visionary leadership, the school underwent an extensive physical and academic transformation:

**Infrastructure and Physical Development:**
- The **main hall** was established and the principal's office was formally structured.
- New **classrooms** were constructed.
- A dedicated **Sports Ground** and **Sports Hall** were built.
- **Primary-level sports facilities** were strengthened and expanded.
- **School toilet facilities** were improved and modernized.
- **Office exterior concrete roads** were completed.
- A **concrete road at the main entrance (party road)** was constructed.
- **Senior student rooms** were built, along with senior student portrait galleries.
- An **Art and Music Room** was renovated.
- A **modern Teachers' Discussion Room** was established.
- A **water pumping system** was installed for sustainable water supply.
- **School discipline pathways** were reinforced.
- Various other **beautification and functional improvement activities** were carried out.

**Academic and Co-Curricular Achievements:**
- The school's **discipline framework** was strengthened, requiring all students from primary to upper secondary levels to participate in sports without exception.
- **Co-curricular programmes** were organized covering sports, aesthetic arts, and academic enrichment.
- New rules, environmental regulations, and sports media were introduced.
- A modern **school library** was systematically improved.
- The school fence was inscribed with the institution's name as a mark of identity and pride.

**Cultural and Musical Honours:**
- In **2016**, the school's **Junior (Kanishtha) Musical Ensemble** won **First Place at the Provincial Level** and went on to represent the province at the **All-Island (All Lanka) level** — a landmark cultural achievement.
- In **2017**, the **Junior Musical Ensemble** once again achieved the **All-Island Honour** at the primary section, bringing national recognition to the school's musical tradition.

**Recognition of Principal Mr. Milinda Padikkorale:**
As a result of the extraordinary service rendered to the school and the wider education system, **Mr. Wipul Milinda Padikkorale** was elevated within the **Sri Lanka Education Administration Service**, receiving a promotion on **23rd November 2017** — a testament to his decade of transformative leadership.

---

## Transitional Administration (2017–2018)

Following Principal Mr. Milinda Padikkorale's promotion, **Mr. L.G. Sarath Kumar** was appointed as Acting Principal and served in this capacity **until 15th May 2018**, ensuring institutional continuity during the transition.

From **15th May 2018**, Mr. L.G. Sarath Kumar formally handed over administration, following which **Mr. K.P. Anuratha** served briefly as Acting Principal from **6th June 2018**.

---

## The Era of Mr. M.A.P. Pushpakumara (from 2018)

From **6th June 2018**, **Mr. M.A.P. Pushpakumara** assumed duties as principal. His tenure began with the school situated on the lower terrain of the Ga/Hapugala area, near the Aganagara lake region of the Galle district. Physical planning and ground-mapping activities were actively undertaken during the early days of his administration.

**Statistical Overview at this Period:**
- **Current teacher count:** 1,286
- **Current student count:** Approximately 63 per class (with 3 administrative functionaries overseeing operations across 9 administrative board members)
- The school aligned its operations under the Hapugala Vidyalaya framework

**Academic Excellence in 2018:**
- In the **A/L (Advanced Level)** subject examinations, one teacher achieved an **A3** grade, while the Commerce stream recorded an **all-subject pass rate of 86%** and the Arts stream recorded **55%** — notable benchmarks in the school's academic history.
- At the **O/L (Ordinary Level)** in **November**, a remarkable **77.17%** pass rate was achieved, with **2 students** from a cohort of **9 students** securing a coveted **Grade A** distinction.
- In the **Grade 5 Scholarship Examination**, **19 students** qualified and passed, among whom only **6 students** succeeded in scoring **above 100 marks** — a noteworthy benchmark.

**Developmental Initiatives under this Administration:**
- A framework was established to systematically identify and nurture talented students from the local area.
- An academic plan was drawn to support students from Grade 6 through Grade 9, Grade 10 through Grade 11, and Grade 12 through Grade 13 progressively.
- Opportunities were created for students across **4 key events** annually covering sports, aesthetics, academics, and co-curricular activities.
- The **School Development Committee** and **Time Management Committee** were actively engaged in resource planning.
- New rules governing environment, water, sports, library, and dining were codified.
- Extracurricular activities were made compulsory for all students from primary to upper secondary.

---

## The School's Broader Legacy and Vision

The Ga/Hapugala Maha Vidyalaya — now standing with **117 years of proud history** — has from its humble coconut-shed beginnings grown into an institution that daily educates the future heirs of the nation in substantial numbers. Its students have competed and won accolades at **national and international levels**, in academics, music, and sports.

The school's historical records close with a prayer and aspiration that encapsulates the spirit of every principal, teacher, parent, and student who has walked its grounds:

> *"That this school, which has for 117 years daily educated those who will inherit and carry forward the nation, will continue to do so for generations yet to come — this is our prayer."*
